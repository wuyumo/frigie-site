Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Frigie does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

All information you put into Frigie — your fridge items, shopping lists, saved recipes, allergies, and settings — is stored only on your device. We have no servers and no account system, so this data never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Camera & Photos

Frigie uses your camera and photo library only to scan grocery receipts and items. Photos are processed on your device using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Frigie does not upload, store, or transmit your photos.

Recipe images

Recipe photos are loaded over HTTPS from TheMealDB.com. These are standard image requests and carry no personal information about you or your device.

No tracking

Frigie contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Frigie does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and revise the date above.
