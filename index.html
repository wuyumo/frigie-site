Frequently asked

How does Frigie know when I'll run out of something?
Frigie isn't an expiry-date tracker. When you set up how many people you feed and how you eat, it estimates how fast your household goes through each item, then counts down how many days of supply you have left. The same carton of milk lasts a family a few days and one person a couple of weeks — Frigie adjusts to your real pace.
How do I add items?
Tap Scan, then photograph a grocery receipt or a single item. Frigie reads it and drops stickers into your fridge automatically — no typing. You can also add items by hand.
How does the shopping list fill itself?
When an item is about to run out (its countdown turns amber or red), Frigie adds it to your shopping list for you. Mark anything "used up" and it's added too. You can share the whole list as an image.
Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Frigie has no account and no cloud — your fridge, lists, and settings never leave your phone. See our Privacy Policy.
Can I track non-food things?
Yes. Create custom pages for household supplies, pet food, or anything else you want to keep stocked.
